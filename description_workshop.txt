# WolfGod RPG - Balanced RPG Mod for Terraria
WolfGod RPG transforms your Terraria experience with an action-based progression system, 13 unique classes, vital stats (hunger, sanity, stamina), items with random stats and evolution, and a custom interface. The focus is on maintaining challenge and natural progression without breaking game balance.

## Core Features
- **13 Action Classes**: Gain XP and level up by using weapons, jumping, running, mining, building, fishing, and more.
- **Vitals System**: Hunger, sanity, and stamina affect your performance.
- **Unique Items**: Random stats and item evolution as you use them.
- **Intuitive Interface**: RPG menu (M), quick stats (R), tabs for classes, progress, and items.
- **Compatibility**: Works in singleplayer, multiplayer, servers, and with other mods.
- **Configurable**: Adjust XP multipliers, difficulty, notifications, and UI scale.

## How to Play
1. Install the mod and enter your world.
2. Press **M** to open the RPG menu.
3. Play normally: XP is gained by performing actions (combat, mining, building, etc).
4. Level up classes, unlock abilities, and optimize your items.
5. Manage hunger and sanity to maintain performance.

## Highlights
- Natural progression, no forced grind.
- Balanced: XP multipliers are limited to preserve challenge.
- Talent and achievement systems planned for the future.
- Open source and active community.
